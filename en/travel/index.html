Safe Flight Protocols

Compression Stockings
Wear 15–20 mmHg on every post-op flight; prevents DVT and eases swelling.
Wheelchair Assist (WCHR)
Request the free WCHR service from your airline at least 48h ahead.
Medication Letter
Carry the signed clinic letter with prescribed medication in your carry-on, never checked luggage.
