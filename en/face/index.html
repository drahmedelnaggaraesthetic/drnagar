Side-by-side comparison
Compare any two techniques
1. Target layer
Hybrid Endolift (1500nm)

Subcutaneous layer and fibrous septa

Cosmetic Threads (Aptos / PCL)

Above the SMAS layer, within the superficial fat

2. Mechanism
Hybrid Endolift (1500nm)

A microscopic laser fiber beneath the skin, with no incisions, to dissolve double-chin fat, tighten the jawline and build collagen.

Cosmetic Threads (Aptos / PCL)

Immediate mechanical lift of the cheeks and jawline while building a supportive collagen sheath.

3. Ideal candidate
Hybrid Endolift (1500nm)

Mild to moderate laxity of the jawline and double chin, for those who prefer tightening without surgery.

Cosmetic Threads (Aptos / PCL)

Mild to moderate descent of the midface and jawline with suitable skin thickness.

4. When it is not advised
Hybrid Endolift (1500nm)

Severe, pendulous skin laxity or prominent neck bands.

Cosmetic Threads (Aptos / PCL)

Very thin skin, or advanced heavy skin laxity.

5. Anesthesia
Hybrid Endolift (1500nm)

Local anesthesia in the clinic

Cosmetic Threads (Aptos / PCL)

Local anesthesia in the clinic

After weight loss
Face readiness calculator after weight loss

Especially after weight-loss injections (GLP-1) or bariatric surgery

A skin-preparation phase only (Olidia or Jovena)
After weight-loss injections, we begin by strengthening the collagen fibers in the skin envelope (Olidia PLLA or Jovena) while checking hemoglobin and albumin, then decide on a lift or Microfat once weight is fully stable.
Annual maintenance plan
A 12-month plan for the face and neck

A quarterly plan (Q1–Q4) to keep results firm and radiant

Q1 (first quarter)
The primary lifting procedure (Hybrid Endolift or Aptos threads) to elevate the jawline and double chin.
Q2 (second quarter)
An Olidia collagen-stimulator session to set the lift and increase dermal density.
Q3 (third quarter)
Nefertiti-technique Botox to relax the downward-pulling neck bands and preserve jawline definition.
Q4 (fourth quarter)
An annual review and a Jovena or Skin Booster session to extend the life of the result.
