The golden range for 4D VASER body sculpting, tummy tuck and postpartum procedures, with the highest safety and definition.

Selective ultrasound technology that dissolves stubborn localized fat with high precision while preserving blood vessels and connective tissue, bringing out waist definition or athletic muscle lines while stimulating skin retraction.

The scar, in full transparency: Very fine incisions (3 – 4 mm) hidden within natural creases and the underwear line, fading gradually.
✓ Ideal candidate: Weight close to ideal (BMI < 30–32) with stubborn localized fat and skin with good elasticity.
When it is not enough on its own: Not sufficient on its own when there is a marked skin apron or moderate to severe abdominal muscle separation (laxity worsens if the excess skin is not removed).

Self-check
Diastasis recti self-check

Lie on your back, lift your head slightly, place your fingers lengthwise on the middle of your abdomen and measure the width of the gap

Normal (< 1.5 fingers)
The gap is within the normal range of the linea alba. If there is abdominal fullness, it is usually localized fat or mild skin laxity that does not call for muscle repair.

When does the procedure become safe after childbirth or weight loss?
Tummy tuck or VASER (6–9 months after childbirth): Earliest tentatively safe date: Saturday, 23 January 2027 (ideally by Friday, 23 April 2027).
Breast lift procedures: Evaluation is available immediately, as there is no active breastfeeding.
