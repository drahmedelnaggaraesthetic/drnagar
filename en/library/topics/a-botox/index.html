Assessment & procedures

Precise Medical and Cosmetic Botox

Why this procedure?

Used in precise anatomical doses to soften forehead lines, frown lines and the eye area without freezing expression, or therapeutically with high effectiveness for excessive sweating (underarms and palms) and chronic migraine.

Realistic expectations

The goal of well-executed Botox is a rested, refreshed look that preserves your natural facial movement; we perform a fine touch-up session after two weeks when needed.

Anesthesia

No anesthesia needed (or a light topical cream)

Scars

No marks at all

Returning to work

Immediate (a 15-minute session)

Returning to exercise

After 24 hours

Result stability

Begins within 3 – 5 days, completes by 14 days, and lasts 4 – 6 months

Suggested stay in days

1

Alternative path

For deep static wrinkles or reduced skin quality

The alternative option

Combining collagen stimulators or regenerative skin care

When do we choose the alternative?

Botox relaxes the muscles that cause wrinkles, while stimulators improve the thickness and elasticity of the skin itself.
