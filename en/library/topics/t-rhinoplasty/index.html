Travel & tests

Preservation Piezo Rhinoplasty (Preservation / Piezo Rhinoplasty)

Suggested stay in nights

8

Hospital stay

One day / one night

Suggested days before flying

7

Travel tips

Flying is safe right after the splint and silicone splints are removed (day 7). Use a saline spray before takeoff and avoid blowing your nose to ease cabin pressure.

Tests before you arrive

CT scan of the sinuses (CT Sinuses) for functional cases • Liver and kidney function and fasting blood sugar

Tests in Cairo

Complete blood count (CBC) + clotting profile (PT, PTT, INR)
