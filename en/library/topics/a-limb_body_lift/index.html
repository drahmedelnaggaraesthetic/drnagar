Arm / Thigh Lift, or Body Lift after Weight Loss

Why this procedure?

After major weight loss, or with advanced laxity of the arms and thighs, elastin fibers lose their ability to retract on their own. Meticulous surgical lifting with VASER assistance removes the excess skin and clearly redefines the limb contour.

Realistic expectations

These procedures trade bothersome loose skin for a fine scar on the inner aspect; early care with silicone sheets and committed local rest during the first two weeks make a major difference to how refined the scar becomes.

Anesthesia

General anesthesia

Scars

A fine incision line placed on the inner arm or the thigh crease to keep it discreet

Returning to work

10 – 14 days

Returning to exercise

Cardio 4–5 weeks • Weights 6–8 weeks

Result stability

Contour settling over 3 – 6 months (with the scar maturing for up to 12 months)

Suggested stay in days

12

Alternative path

In cases of very mild laxity

The alternative option

VASER liposuction with skin-tightening stimulation

When do we choose the alternative?

Considered only when laxity is superficial and very limited on manual assessment of skin elasticity.
