Assessment & procedures

Upper or Lower Eyelid Lift

Why this procedure?

A precise procedure with quick recovery that removes the tired excess skin of the upper lid and repositions or removes the fat pockets under the eye, restoring the eye's openness and natural freshness without changing the character of your features.

Realistic expectations

Eyelid surgery treats excess skin and fatty bags with remarkable effectiveness, while pigmented dark circles or deep hollowing may need added nanofat or complementary skin care.

Anesthesia

Comfortable local anesthesia (or light sedation)

Scars

Completely hidden inside the upper eyelid crease, or just below the lash line / from inside the lid

Returning to work

7 – 10 days (after removal of the fine sutures)

Returning to exercise

After 2 – 3 weeks

Result stability

Clear improvement within 3 weeks and full settling within 2 – 3 months

Suggested stay in days

7

Alternative path

A complementary protocol for the eye area

The alternative option

Nano-Fat injections or micro-Botox

When do we choose the alternative?

Improves lower eyelid skin quality and softens expression lines around the eyes in natural harmony.
