Recovery

Facelift and eyelid lift · The First Week

Stage summary

The fine sutures around the eyelids and ears are removed, and bruising recedes quickly.

What is normal at this stage

Eyelid sutures are removed (days 5–7) and face and ear sutures (days 7–10), smoothly. • A major reduction in acute swelling, with bruising turning light yellow. • The eyes feel more comfortable, and a fresh firmness begins to show along the jawline and neck.

Daily movement

Quiet outdoor walks with sunglasses and a hat for protection

Desk work

Light remote tasks from day 7, without prolonged eye strain

Driving

Between days 7 and 10, once vision is clear and neck turning is smooth

Cardio

Deferred to weeks three to four

Gym

No weights and no bending

Medical face garment

During sleep and at home · Keep wearing it at night or at home during week two to support the jawline.

Incision hygiene and hair washing

With utmost gentleness · Wash hair with lukewarm water and a gentle shampoo, keeping the hot hairdryer away from the ears.

Care tip

Do not apply makeup over the incision lines before the sutures are removed and the surgeon approves; after approval, gentle products can be used.
