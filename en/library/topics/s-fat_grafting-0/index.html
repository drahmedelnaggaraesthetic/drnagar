Recovery

Autologous fat transfer · The First Days

Stage summary

The golden rule: no direct pressure on the treated area, so the transferred fat cells can establish their blood supply.

What is normal at this stage

Fullness and swelling slightly beyond the expected final size (intentional and normal). • Muscle soreness and light pink fluid from the liposuction areas in the first 48 hours. • Sleep in a position that keeps all pressure off the treated area.

Daily movement

Light walking around the house, 5 minutes every two hours, without sitting directly on the treated area

Desk work

Rest at home, avoiding sitting directly on the treated area

Driving

Off limits to protect the transferred fat cells

Cardio

Strictly off limits to avoid burning off the transferred fat

Gym

Strictly off limits

The open garment + pressure offloading

24 hours a day · Compresses only the liposuction areas, and is cut away or fully smooth over the treated area with no pressure at all.

No smoking, and healthy nutrition

A basic requirement for lasting results · Complete abstinence from smoking and pressure, because the fat cells are building their capillaries now.

Care tip

The first 3 to 4 weeks are the fat-cell stabilization period; any direct pressure or smoking reduces the fat survival rate.
