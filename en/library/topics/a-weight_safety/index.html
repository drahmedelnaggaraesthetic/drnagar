Assessment & procedures

Preparing your weight and medical safety first, before body contouring

Why this procedure?

Plastic surgery is designed to sculpt and harmonize the body; it is not a weight-loss tool. At a BMI of 35 or above, the risks of anesthesia, fluid accumulation and poor wound healing rise, and sculpting precision falls. Starting with a structured therapeutic nutrition plan toward a BMI of 30–32 gives you the highest level of medical safety and an exceptional, lasting sculpting and tightening result.

Realistic expectations

Medical transparency requires us to place your safety above any quick procedure; waiting a few months to bring weight down safely multiplies the beauty of the result and reduces the chance of needing any later revision.
