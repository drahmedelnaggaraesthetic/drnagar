Recovery

Autologous fat transfer · Weeks 3–4

Stage summary

Excellent fat-cell stabilization, with normal sitting and light cardio beginning at the end of the month.

What is normal at this stage

A very natural softness and flexibility in the feel of the treated area. • Most of the water swelling resolves and the permanent share of fat stabilizes (usually 60–75%). • A striking harmony between the sculpted liposuction areas and the fuller area.

Daily movement

Full daily movement, with a gradual return to normal sitting at the end of week 4

Desk work

Full desk work with growing comfort

Driving

Natural, comfortable driving

Cardio

Very light cardio begins in week four, without aggressive calorie burning

Gym

Gym and weights deferred to week six

Transitioning to normal sitting

After completing 4 weeks · Begin comfortable sitting on soft surfaces and reduce the garment to 12 hours a day.

Skin moisturizing and incision-site care

Daily · Ongoing moisturizing keeps the skin supple and soft.

Care tip

Avoid excessive cardio or rapid weight loss during the first two months so you do not lose some of the newly stabilized fat.
