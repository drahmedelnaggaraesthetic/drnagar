Assessment & procedures

Cosmetic Threads with a Skin-Quality Protocol

Why this procedure?

Suitable for moderate descent of the cheeks and jawline when you do not want surgery now; the mechanical threads (Aptos / PCL) give an immediate lift with gradual collagen stimulation along the thread path.

Realistic expectations

Threads are a medium-term solution (usually one to one and a half years) and do not remove excess skin; if laxity is severe or the neck is clearly sagging, a SMAS facelift is the more honest and durable option.

Anesthesia

Local anesthesia in the clinic

Scars

No scarring (fine needle entry points that fade within days)

Returning to work

2 – 4 days

Returning to exercise

After 1 – 2 weeks

Result stability

Immediate lift with collagen improvement over two months; results last 12 – 18 months

Suggested stay in days

3

Alternative path

The longest-lasting or the most conservative alternative

The alternative option

Hybrid Endolift, or Deep Face and Neck Lift (SMAS)

When do we choose the alternative?

We choose the most suitable option during the examination, based on skin thickness and the weight of the lax tissues.
