Recovery

Abdominoplasty · The Second Week

Stage summary

Standing fully upright and returning to desk work and safe driving.

What is normal at this stage

Posture is usually fully upright by the end of week two, with easier, more flexible walking. • The surface layer of the incision line and navel heals, with brief tingling as sensation returns. • Noticeable swelling just above the incision line that increases slightly in the evening and settles with rest.

Daily movement

Comfortable daily movement and relaxed outdoor walks of 15 – 20 minutes

Desk work

Return to desk work between days 10 – 14 with movement breaks

Driving

Once the back is straight and you can brake quickly

Cardio

Deferred until weeks four to six are complete

Gym

No lifting over 5 kg and no straining the abdomen

Supportive compression garment

24 hours a day · Wearing the garment while sitting and working supports a flat abdomen and reduces evening swelling.

Starting scar care

Once 100% closed · After تأكد الطبيب of الإغلاق الكامل يبدأ بروتوكول جل أو slides السيليكون.

Care tip

Stand and walk for two minutes every hour during desk work; uninterrupted sitting increases fluid pooling above the incision line.
