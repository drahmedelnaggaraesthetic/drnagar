Nose

Fact 1: airy silicone splints instead of the painful traditional packing

Details

The era of long cotton packing that stuck to the nasal lining and caused severe pain on removal is over. Today we use soft, hollow medical silicone splints (Doyle Airway Splints) with an internal airway that allows partial breathing after surgery. They do not adhere to tissue, so they are removed in the clinic within seconds, calmly and with virtually no pain.
