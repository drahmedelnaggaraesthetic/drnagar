Recovery

Abdominoplasty with body contouring · The First Week

Stage summary

The drain is usually removed and flank and back pain eases, with less of a bend.

What is normal at this stage

Fluid output decreases and the drain is usually removed between days five and ten. • Gradual improvement in flank and back pain, with the walking bend beginning to ease. • Mild firmness in the contoured areas that later responds to gentle lymphatic massage.

Daily movement

Frequent relaxed walks around the house, more comfortably

Desk work

Limited screen-based tasks by the end of the week, as energy allows

Driving

Deferred until the back is fully straight

Cardio

Off limits for now

Gym

Strictly off limits

The full-coverage garment + flank foam

24 hours a day · Use shaping foam over the flanks and back as directed to keep the skin smooth.

Gentle lymphatic massage for the back and flanks

Away from the abdominal incision · Lymphatic drainage of the contoured areas with the surgeon's clearance, staying completely clear of the abdominal incision line.

Care tip

Feeling somewhat tired in the first week is entirely normal; give your body a genuine chance to repair without rushing.
