Assessment & procedures

Mini abdominoplasty with VASER waist sculpting

Why this procedure?

This procedure combines VASER fat dissolving and sculpting of the upper abdomen and waist with tightening and removal of mild to moderate laxity in the lower abdomen below the navel only, offering faster recovery and a shorter scar than a full abdominoplasty.

Realistic expectations

The final choice between a mini and a full abdominoplasty is made at the in-person examination; if laxity extends above the navel or the muscle separation is extensive, a full abdominoplasty gives a far better result.

Anesthesia

General or regional anesthesia

Scars

A shorter, very low scar (similar to a C-section line) with no incision around the navel

Returning to work

7 – 10 days

Returning to exercise

Cardio 3–4 weeks • Full gym 6–8 weeks

Result stability

Within 3 – 5 months

Suggested stay in days

9

Alternative path

Two alternatives, decided at the in-person examination

The alternative option

VASER sculpting with skin-tightening devices, or a full abdominoplasty

When do we choose the alternative?

If skin elasticity is excellent, VASER alone may be enough; if the laxity extends above the navel, a full abdominoplasty is the better choice.
