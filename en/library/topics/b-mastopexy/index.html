Breast Lift

Scientific name

Mastopexy (Breast Lift)

The procedure in detail

Reshaping the breast tissue, returning the nipple to its youthful anatomical position and removing loose skin after pregnancy, breastfeeding or weight loss; it can be combined with an implant or your own fat when extra upper fullness is desired.

Target layers

Skin • Breast tissue

The right candidate

Nipple descent and loose breast skin after breastfeeding or weight loss.

When is it not enough on its own?

Not performed during breastfeeding; it is best to wait 6 months after complete weaning for breast size and tissue to stabilize.

Scar placement

Around the areola with a fine vertical line (Lollipop), or with a line hidden in the lower crease (Anchor), depending on the degree of sagging.

Anesthesia

General anesthesia

Hospital stay

Same-day discharge or one overnight stay

Recovery

Desk work 7 – 10 days • Support bra 6 weeks • Gym 6 weeks
