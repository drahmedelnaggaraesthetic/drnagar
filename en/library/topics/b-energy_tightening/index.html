Assisted Skin-Tightening with Sculpting

Scientific name

Energy-Assisted Skin Tightening (J-Plasma / RF / Endolift)

The procedure in detail

Controlled thermal energy delivered directly beneath the skin right after VASER sculpting, to shrink the fibrous septa and stimulate additional skin retraction in borderline mild to moderate laxity.

Target layers

Collagen fibers beneath the skin

The right candidate

Early mild to moderate laxity of the abdomen, arms or thighs, for those who do not need skin excision.

When is it not enough on its own?

An honest medical fact: no energy device can remove severely hanging skin or replace a surgical abdominoplasty in advanced laxity.

Scar placement

Used through the same fine VASER incisions (3 – 4 mm), with no additional cuts.

Anesthesia

Combined with the VASER procedure's anesthesia

Hospital stay

Same stay as the sculpting (same-day discharge)

Recovery

Same recovery as VASER (5 – 7 days of work • 6 weeks of compression garment)
