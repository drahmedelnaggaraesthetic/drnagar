Recovery

VASER Body Contouring · The Second Week

Stage summary

Most bruising fades, softness increases, and the first sculpting lines begin to appear.

What is normal at this stage

Most superficial bruising fades and the acute swelling subsides noticeably. • Temporary firm areas or mild itching under the skin; a normal stage of skin retraction. • Early definition of the waist and flanks, with mild evening swelling that settles by morning.

Daily movement

Daily routines and regular brisk walking without strain

Desk work

Full desk work with short standing breaks

Driving

Fully comfortable and safe driving

Cardio

Very light cardio at the end of week two, as comfort allows

Gym

Weights deferred until week four is complete

Adjusting the compression garment size

24 hours a day · Tightening the garment or moving to a smaller size as swelling subsides maintains the quality of the contouring.

Lymphatic massage + incision-site care

2 sessions per week · Release any remaining firmness and begin scar-care creams for the tiny incision marks.

Care tip

Cut back on salt and pickles and increase healthy protein; excess sodium directly doubles evening swelling.
