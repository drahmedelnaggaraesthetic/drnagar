Recovery

Abdominoplasty · The Second Month

Stage summary

Moderate cardio and light weights for the limbs, while phasing out the garment.

What is normal at this stage

A clearly flatter abdomen with good abdominal-wall stability, and only minor swelling after demanding days. • Gradual improvement in skin sensation below the navel. • Free swimming once complete healing of the incision and navel is confirmed.

Daily movement

Full daily life and light jogging

Desk work

All office and field work

Driving

With no restrictions

Cardio

Moderate-intensity cardio (treadmill / bike / elliptical)

Gym

Light weights for the limbs only; abdominal exercises deferred until after week 8

Compression garment

12 hours, then stopped (weeks 6–8) · The garment is reduced to 12 daytime hours, then stopped according to the follow-up assessment.

Silicone + sun protection

Daily routine · Protecting the scar line from direct sun is essential to prevent darkening.

Care tip

When training with light weights for the limbs, breathe smoothly and avoid holding your breath or bracing your abdomen hard during lifts.
