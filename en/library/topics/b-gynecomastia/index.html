Gynecomastia Treatment and Chest Sculpting for Men

Scientific name

Gynecomastia 4D Surgery

The procedure in detail

Removing fat and sculpting the borders of the chest muscle with 4D VASER, with excision of the firm fibrous gland through a tiny incision at the lower edge of the areola, for a flat, defined masculine chest; skin tightening is added in advanced grades.

Target layers

The fibrous gland • Fat • Skin

The right candidate

Men with chest fullness (grades 1 to 4), whether fatty, glandular, or after major weight loss.

When is it not enough on its own?

Liposuction alone, without removing the fibrous gland, leaves a bulge behind the nipple; combining VASER with gland excision is the standard.

Scar placement

A very fine micro-incision at the lower areola edge that becomes almost invisible (or tightening lines in severe grade 4).

Anesthesia

General anesthesia, or local anesthesia with sedation

Hospital stay

Same-day discharge

Recovery

Desk work 3 – 5 days • Cardio 2 weeks • Chest exercises 4 – 6 weeks
