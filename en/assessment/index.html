Dissolving and sculpting localized fat while bringing out muscle definition or the waistline

2. The degree of laxity it suits

Firm skin, or very mild laxity only

Moderate to severe laxity / skin apron / muscle weakness

Tiny (3–4 mm) incisions that are not visible

A low line hidden below the underwear line

Dynamic 4D VASER Body Contouring

Permanent, provided weight and lifestyle stay stable

Full Abdominoplasty with Muscle Repair and Waist Sculpting

Long-lasting and stable (affected only by pregnancy or major weight change)

7. The medical basis for choosing

Dynamic 4D VASER Body Contouring

The first choice when the problem is localized fat with good skin elasticity.

Full Abdominoplasty with Muscle Repair and Waist Sculpting

The only definitive option when there is clear skin excess or muscle separation.

Points worth preparing before booking (6):
  • It is best to keep weight stable for 3–6 months before sculpting and tightening procedures to secure a stable result.
  • Stopping smoking and vaping 3–4 weeks before the procedure is essential to protect blood supply and wound healing.
  • If a pregnancy is planned soon, it is best to postpone abdominoplasty and muscle repair until the family is complete.
  • Controlling blood sugar and blood pressure and treating any anemia (raising hemoglobin) is an absolute priority before scheduling surgery.
  • All medications and supplements (such as aspirin, vitamin E and omega-3) must be reviewed and stopped at a safe time under the doctor's supervision.
  • Setting aside recovery time and committing to the compression garment and follow-up visits make up half the success of the aesthetic result.
